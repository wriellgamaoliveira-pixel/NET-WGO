Every hero in W-G-O NetNavi starts their journey at the same place: **Level 0 — Iniciante** (Beginner). As you complete daily habits and missions and accumulate XP, your hero grows stronger and climbs through the level system. Leveling up isn't just a number — it triggers a special hero pose and celebration animation that marks how far you've come.

## How Levels Work

The level system is straightforward: for every **1,000 XP** you earn, your hero gains **one level**. Your current level is always calculated from your total XP using this formula:

```text
level = floor(totalXP / 1000)
```

This means it doesn't matter how you spread your XP earnings over time — your level always reflects your total progress. Wallas is currently at **85 XP (Level 0)** and Melynna is at **83 XP (Level 0)**. Both heroes are building momentum toward their first level-up!

## XP Progress Bar

The progress bar on your hero's profile shows how far you are through your current level. It fills up based on the XP you've earned since the last level threshold:

```text
progress = (totalXP mod 1000) / 1000 × 100%
```

For example, if your hero has 1,350 XP total, the bar shows **35%** of the way to Level 2 — because 350 of the 1,000 XP needed for the next level have already been earned.

## Level Thresholds

Use this table to see how XP maps to levels. Every level always requires exactly 1,000 XP to reach from the one before it.

| Level | XP Required to Reach This Level | Total XP Needed |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 1 | 1,000 | 1,000 |
| 2 | 1,000 | 2,000 |
| 3 | 1,000 | 3,000 |
| 4 | 1,000 | 4,000 |
| 5 | 1,000 | 5,000 |
| 10 | 1,000 | 10,000 |

## Level-Up Celebration

When your hero crosses a 1,000 XP threshold, W-G-O NetNavi plays a **hero pose and celebration animation** to mark the moment. This is your reward for consistent effort — a visual shout-out to your hard work before the adventure continues.

## Level Names

At Level 0, every hero carries the title **Iniciante** (Beginner). Level names and titles for higher levels are planned for a future update — so as the app grows, reaching Level 5 or Level 10 will come with its own unique hero title to show off.
