W-G-O NetNavi centers on two heroes: **Wallas** and **Melynna**. Each hero lives in their own fully independent profile — complete with a personal XP total, level rank, completed mission history, and a 3D avatar slot. Progress is never shared or merged between the two; every XP point each child earns belongs entirely to them. This design keeps siblings motivated individually while letting the whole family celebrate together.

## The Level System

Both heroes start at **Level 0 — Iniciante**. Every **1,000 XP** earned advances a hero by exactly one level. Levels are calculated independently — Wallas reaching Level 1 has no effect on Melynna's progress, and vice versa. There is no maximum level; the system is designed to grow alongside the children as their habits mature.

## Switching Between Heroes

To view or interact with a different hero's profile, tap the **character name tab** at the top of the main screen. The app immediately switches context: the theme color updates, the mission list refreshes to show that hero's active tasks, and the XP progress bar reflects their personal total. No login or PIN is required to switch — the tab is always visible.

## 3D Avatars

Each hero profile includes a **3D avatar slot** that displays a GLB model on the profile screen. If you have not imported a custom avatar yet, the app automatically shows a **default placeholder avatar** so the profile always looks complete. To import a custom GLB file, open the hero's profile settings and tap **Import Avatar**. The new model replaces the placeholder immediately.
