Every great hero needs a challenge, and in W-G-O NetNavi the challenges come in the form of three mischievous villains. Preguix, Desanimax, and Distrax lurk in the background, waiting to appear when your hero skips habits, loses focus, or spends too long in front of a screen. They're not here to scare you — they're here to remind you that your hero is stronger than any obstacle, and getting back on track is always possible.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="😴 Preguix — The Laziness Villain" icon="bed">
    **Preguix** feeds on *preguiça* — laziness. This sleepy troublemaker slips in when your hero starts skipping the daily habits that keep a hero strong: missing baths, skipping meals, or not getting proper sleep night after night.

    **What triggers Preguix:**
    - Repeatedly skipping bath time 🚿
    - Missing meals like lunch or dinner 🍽️🌙
    - Not napping or failing to sleep through the night 😴🛌

    **How to defeat Preguix:**
    Jump back into your routine! Take a bath, sit down for a meal, and hit the pillow at the right time. Every completed habit weakens Preguix a little more. String together a few good days in a row and this villain will retreat — at least until laziness creeps back in. Your hero's daily streak is your best weapon against Preguix.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="🥱 Desanimax — The Disinterest Villain" icon="ghost">
    **Desanimax** thrives on *desinteresse* — disinterest and disconnection. This grey, faded villain shows up when your hero hasn't engaged with missions, hasn't been to school, or has gone quiet on activities for too long.

    **What triggers Desanimax:**
    - Not engaging with school activities or skipping the school day bonus ✅🏫
    - Ignoring available missions and activities in the app
    - Long stretches without completing any clinic or school bonus goals

    **How to defeat Desanimax:**
    Re-engage! Head to school and give it your best effort to earn the bonus +5 XP. Pick up the Bob's Goods drawing — even just starting it earns +2 XP and sends Desanimax running. Show Desanimax that your hero is curious, active, and ready to take on new challenges. Interest is contagious, and once you get going, this villain doesn't stand a chance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="📺 Distrax — The Distraction Villain" icon="tv">
    **Distrax** is powered by *distração* — distraction. This flashy, screen-obsessed villain appears when too many hours pile up in front of the TV or video games, racking up XP penalties that chip away at your hero's progress.

    **What triggers Distrax:**
    - Watching TV for multiple hours, each hour costing −1 XP 📺
    - Playing video games or using the computer for extended sessions, also −1 XP per hour 🎮
    - Accumulated screen-time penalties that noticeably lower your hero's XP total

    **How to defeat Distrax:**
    Put down the remote or controller and redirect your energy. Trade screen time for a productive habit — brush your teeth, take a bath, or work on the Bob's Goods drawing. Each positive action you complete not only earns XP to replace what was lost, but it proves to Distrax that your hero is in control of their own time. Balance is the key: a little screen time is fine, but your hero's mission matters more.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
Villains in W-G-O NetNavi are **motivational tools, not punishments**. When one of them shows up on your screen, think of it as a friendly nudge — the game is saying *"Hey, your hero is ready to get back on track!"* Every villain can be defeated, and defeating them is just another part of the adventure.
</Tip>
