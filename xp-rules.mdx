Every action your hero takes in W-G-O NetNavi has a score attached to it. Good habits and completed missions earn you XP, while things like too much screen time or ignoring a parent's guidance can cost you points. Use this page as your complete reference for how XP is gained and lost — knowing the rules is the first step to leveling up fast.

## Rewards

Complete daily habits and special missions to stack up positive XP. Each activity below adds to your hero's total and brings you one step closer to your next level.

| Activity | XP Earned |
|---|---|
| 🍽️ Eat lunch | +5 XP |
| 🌙 Eat dinner | +5 XP |
| 😴 Take a nap | +5 XP |
| 🛌 Sleep through the night (sleep at night, wake up only in the morning) | +10 XP |
| 🏫 Go to school | +5 XP |
| ✅ Good behavior + activities at school *(bonus)* | +5 XP extra |
| 🏥 Go to the clinic | +5 XP |
| ✅ Good behavior + activities at clinic *(bonus)* | +5 XP extra |
| 🚿 Take a bath | +5 XP |
| 🪥 Brush teeth | +5 XP |
| 💊 Take medicine | +5 XP |
| 🎨 Bob's Goods drawing — full completion | +50 XP |
| 🎨 Bob's Goods drawing — start only | +2 XP |
| 🎨 Bob's Goods drawing — completion after starting | +48 XP *(total: +50 XP)* |

### The Bob's Goods Mission

Bob's Goods is a special drawing mission in W-G-O NetNavi. Your hero visits Bob's shop and takes on a creative challenge: complete a full drawing to earn a big reward. You don't have to finish it all at once — if you start the drawing, you immediately earn **+2 XP** for picking up the pencil. When you come back and finish the drawing later, you earn the remaining **+48 XP**, bringing your total reward to **+50 XP**. It's one of the biggest single-mission payouts in the game, so it's worth seeing through to the end!

## Penalties

Some choices can reduce your hero's XP. These penalties are tracked automatically for screen time and applied by a guardian for behavior incidents.

| Activity | XP Lost |
|---|---|
| 📺 Watching TV | −1 XP per hour |
| 🎮 Playing video games or using a computer | −1 XP per hour |
| ⚠️ Inappropriate response to a parent, disobedience, or shouting after guidance | −1 XP *(guardian-confirmed only)* |

<Warning>
TV and video game penalties **accumulate over time** — every hour of screen time costs your hero 1 XP. Two hours of TV is −2 XP, three hours is −3 XP, and so on. Keep an eye on the clock so your screen time doesn't chip away at the XP you worked hard to earn.
</Warning>

<Note>
Behavior penalties — such as disobedience or shouting after guidance — are **never applied automatically**. A parent or guardian must review and confirm the incident before any XP is deducted. This ensures that penalties are always fair and based on what actually happened.
</Note>
