W-G-O NetNavi is a family-focused Android app that rewards children Wallas and Melynna for completing daily habits, missions, and good behavior. Every bath, meal, nap, school visit, and act of kindness earns XP — bringing each hero closer to the next level. The app runs fully offline, displays interactive 3D avatars, and lets a parent or AI assistant push updates through simple package files.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Your Hero">
    Open the app and tap **Wallas** or **Melynna** on the main screen. Each character has their own XP total, level, history, and 3D avatar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Daily Habits">
    Each completed habit — lunch, bath, brushing teeth, nap, medicine — earns XP automatically when a parent logs it. Special missions like *Bob's Goods* reward up to 50 XP.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Level Up">
    Every 1,000 XP earns a new level. Watch the progress bar fill and celebrate each milestone with a hero pose animation!
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive AI Updates">
    At the end of the day, an AI assistant can generate a `.wgo` update package with the day's XP history. Import it in the **AI Updates** tab — preview the changes, confirm, and your data stays current.
  </Step>
</Steps>
